# Coverage safeguards — autonomous methods, accountable completion

Read this for broad job discovery and repeat market research. Do not require the user to fill out this document. These safeguards describe evidence to leave, not a fixed tool sequence, number of companies, queries, platforms, subagents, or recommendations. A supplied-posting review may declare a narrower scope and omit market discovery.

## 1. Map the search space before pruning

Create a compact, task-specific coverage map from the user's approved role families, actual work, career stage, geography, accepted employment types, plausible employer/industry groups, active candidates, and known gaps. Do not enumerate a Cartesian product or import the author's personal career restrictions. Maintain exploration beyond previously successful employers.

A branch can be addressed by a shared query or source if the actual results cover it. A branch's status is checked, open gap, deliberately deferred, or not applicable. A check needs an observable source/query result; not applicable needs a current user/task reason. No results and no access are different. Do not treat a platform search hit count as inspected listings.

Plans can evolve when new evidence changes priorities. Preserve a short change reason; do not delete inconvenient branches or shrink a denominator to manufacture completion. New plausible branches can be added without user permission to research; this does not authorize changing user hard constraints.

## 2. Cover complementary failure modes

Account for employer-led discovery, job-platform discovery, and duty/title-alternative discovery where relevant. Methods may overlap or substitute when equivalent coverage is actually obtained. Do not demand all named platforms every run. A single narrow source requires a material limitation or a justified task-specific reason, not a claim of broad coverage. Mirrored copies of one origin are not independent counter-search evidence.

Check newer/changed jobs AND older still-open, rolling, reposted, and deadline-extended roles. On repeats, use confirmed prior progress plus an overlap matched to elapsed time and source indexing, not a universal lookback window. Search-result date filters cannot establish all currently open jobs.

Inspect individual roles within mixed campaigns; do not discard a whole campaign from its header. Missing experience in memory is not proof of inability. Record a consequential exclusion's actual requirement or scoped user decision, not only a title or vague fit label.

## 3. Challenge the first pass before stopping

Choose counter-searches based on the most likely omissions: a different source family, different employer/industry set, duty-based phrasing, older-open inventory, or a role-specific employer page. Reusing the same snippets with slightly different words does not test the blind spot.

Revisit high-impact uncertain exclusions: missing/ambiguous seniority, mixed hiring rounds, unfamiliar titles, preferred versus required qualifications, and inaccessible details. Verified hard conflicts do not need pointless repeated checking.

When a meaningful new fit appears in the counter-search, expand the branch that exposed it. When feasible, challenge the new boundary again. Do not stop only because a target number of recommendations has been found. Equally, do not run infinite duplicate searches when no untested consequential branch remains.

## 4. Prevent repeated neglect

With authorized private continuity, carry forward branch identity, last attempted/checked time, what failed, and the reason/event/date for revisiting. Increase the priority of aging unanswered gaps; successive runs must not endlessly favor familiar employers. Set revisit timing in light of deadlines, volatility, user needs, and available resources, not a universal fixed cadence.

If a branch is deferred again, keep the original gap age and record why. On the next run inspect both urgent updates and the oldest consequential unexplored areas. New data may change the ordering, but not erase the old gap.

Without durable storage, keep the current run's receipt in available working context and disclose the continuity limitation when material. Do not claim cross-run coverage was restored. Do not require a new database or expose personal logs in the public repository.

## 5. Leave a small evidence receipt

The smallest sufficient form is a short internal table or structured record with:
- scope and planned branches;
- observed checks with source/query identity and evidence reference;
- independent counter-search and consequential exclusion review;
- open/deferred gaps and next revisit trigger;
- stop reason, actual loaded skill version when observable, and save result.

An optional synthetic shape is in assets/coverage-receipt.example.json. The host can use another equivalent form. This is developer/agent state, never an intake form or mandatory user output. Store observations and concise decision grounds, not hidden reasoning.

Evidence references must point to actual tool observations or provided sources. Self-authored receipts are claims about execution; they do not independently prove browsing or market recall. Stronger verification compares them with host tool traces when available. No record format can prove national completeness.

## 6. Completion and the public brief

A broad run may say its planned scope was checked only when the active plan is reconciled, complementary relevant routes and old-open jobs are accounted for, material counter-search findings are addressed, consequential exclusions are justified, and no due material branch has been silently deferred. Explain material remaining uncertainty instead of using a vague complete label.

Resource limits, source blocks, missing continuity, or untested branches produce a useful partial result, not a recommendation ban. A verified individual recommendation can remain valid during partial market coverage. An unverified receipt cannot become a verified audit claim.

Keep default reports short and change-focused. Do not print this checklist, coverage map, or technical counts routinely. If a gap affects the user's action, add one plain-language sentence. If asked about scope, use the actual receipt, not reconstructed counts. No arbitrary top-N recommendation cutoff.

## 7. Reconcile the whole discovery-to-action path

Treat omission at collection, screening and report compression separately. Keep candidate
IDs from observed results, the status/reason of serious candidates, and the IDs displayed
or available in an actual current list. A promising uninspected role is a gap, not an
exclusion. No evidence-linked disposition means the screening queue is unfinished.
A recommended actionable role missing from both visible report and a real accessible
list is a report omission. Auditing only search queries misses these failures.

For material counter-search discoveries record follow-through, not just “audit done”.
Do not sample only already-recommended candidates. Review plausible false negatives
from uncertain title/seniority/experience/route filters. A fresh signal in an unfamiliar
industry can expand the branch without adopting that industry as a new hard preference.

A zero-new-job result is not a quantitative recall guarantee. A local receipt validator
can detect missing fields, unresolved branches and lost candidates; it cannot authenticate
model-written evidence IDs or prove that the open web is exhausted. Where host traces
exist, compare referenced observations with actual source/tool events. Otherwise mark
assurance as self_reported or source_linked, never trace_verified.

## 8. Source boundaries and truncated inventories

When an employer/portal exposes pagination, a cursor or a filtered inventory, record
the actual inspected range and whether the end was reached. Search engine top hits
are discovery, not a platform's full inventory. If pagination is inaccessible, use a
complementary route or retain that gap; do not mark the whole platform checked.
An inventory may seed a changing employer frontier without any fixed company quota.
Rotate neglected industry/role branches while protecting real imminent deadlines;
refresh the frontier as employers and titles change. A source-only new fit is evidence
that the other route may have blind spots, not proof of a newly posted vacancy.

Refresh time-sensitive status before an actionable recommendation and before selected
application planning. Choose recency by deadline/volatility and actual source state,
not a universal cache lifetime. Source publication time, first discovery, last checked
time and actual deadline are separate. If a job cannot be reverified, preserve the
last observed fact with its time rather than presenting it as newly confirmed.
