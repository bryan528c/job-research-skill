# Adaptive research and evidence

## Freedom with meaningful checkpoints

The coverage safeguard contract linked by SKILL.md applies to broad discovery. Search methods and order are adaptive. Employer lists, platform browsing, duty-based searches, source-specific queries, authorized APIs, public feeds, code-assisted extraction, and available subagents are options, not compulsory stages. Equivalent coverage must be evidenced; "optional method" does not mean a relevant blind spot can be silently ignored. Do not require every source on every run. Do not fix company counts or tool-call quotas as a proxy for quality.

Choose routes that increase the chance of finding useful opportunities. A weak result set calls for different terms, adjacent task labels, neglected industries, and employer-led checks. Unexpected promising roles may be investigated without asking permission merely to explore. Do not redefine the user's career target or hard constraints without their decision.

First run: build broad current-opening awareness. Repeat runs: detect new/changed roles and revisit active, old-but-open, and neglected areas. Include a recovery overlap after a missed run; the old fixed seven-day window is not a universal rule. Search publication date, discovery date, and last checked date are different.

Before concluding that there is little to apply to, check plausible blind spots through a different route. A short list is not a stop condition. Equally, infinite repeated queries are not a quality goal. Stop when the useful planned scope and material blind spots are addressed, or actual execution constraints require a checkpoint. Record the scope inspected; never shrink the plan after the fact to inflate completion.

## Source handling

Use the latest applicable official employer/role source where possible, supplemented by employer-posted platforms and other reliable sources. Search snippets are discovery leads; they do not prove a working application route. Failed access does not prove closure. Several mirrored listings are not several independent confirmations.

If an important condition conflicts, preserve both observations and resolve through the most authoritative current role-specific material. If still unresolved, mark that condition—not the user's ability—as unknown.

Use available image/page inspection for image-only job descriptions rather than inferring unseen text. Follow the host's document-handling requirements. Do not bypass authentication, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, source restrictions, or tool permissions.

## Listing identity and dates

Distinguish legal employer, hiring campaign, individual role, and source listing ID. Group-wide new-graduate eligibility does not establish that each role accepts graduates. One URL can contain multiple roles; multiple URLs can represent the same role.

Retain discovered-at, published-at when known, last-verified-at, deadline, source timezone, and whether the deadline has a verified time. Unknown time stays unknown. If converting a deadline into the user's timezone, preserve the original. If the timezone itself is unclear and affects action, disclose it instead of silently converting. Date-only deadlines must not be sorted using invented times.

Separate the discovery page, official information page, and actual application route. Application route matters only as far as the user cares or it affects execution; do not make a universal ban on platform applications.

## Suitability

Extract the work that dominates the role, not every bullet equally. Match with direct or transferable evidence. Do not equate degree title with specialized practice. Conversely, do not require professional revenue, employer tenure, or credentials the entry-level employer does not require.

A gap in a preferred qualification or unfamiliar industry jargon is not automatically disqualifying. A verified unfulfilled essential requirement is different. A listing requiring native-level language is not equivalent to one merely preferring English. Respect the individual's actual conditions, not this skill author's personal preferences.

Uncertainty has consequences:
- Blocking: could reverse basic eligibility or conflict with a stated hard condition. Keep in "Check first".
- Action-relevant: affects preparation or willingness, not proven disqualification. State the risk and choose a proportionate next step.
- Low-consequence: do not turn every unspecified detail into a questionnaire or indefinite hold.

Do not use unverified assumptions to fill hard eligibility. Do not refuse useful preparation because a nonessential detail is absent. Do not infer rejection reasons or hidden school filters from outcomes.

## Continuation record

Record concise observable actions, source identifiers, inspected scope, decision evidence, and unresolved gaps. Do not record hidden chain-of-thought. When an authorized state store exists, retain enough to resume without starting over. Distinguish search hits, unique listings, detailed reviews, and recommendations; do not report a total you did not measure.

Run completion and candidate verification are independent. A partial market search can still produce a thoroughly verified recommendation; a completed search plan can leave a role-specific question unresolved. Show limitations that change the user's decision, and keep routine audit detail out of the brief report.
